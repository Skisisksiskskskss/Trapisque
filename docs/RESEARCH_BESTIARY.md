[W:Soul Corrupted] M

## Cross-species patterns worth keeping in mind (observations, not canon)
- Two hostile ecologies: **Sifters** (plant/animal surface fauna, colourful, support/ranged/melee roles) vs **Sculkers** (sculk hunters of the Carapace, soul-fire, afterimages, dashes).
- Recurring support mechanic: beams that **buff allies** (Sprout → Harmonizer), countered by killing the linked ally.
- Souls appear as **projectiles, fire and pools** (Scavenger, Sculk Monstrosity, Twisted Warden) — the hostile side of the soul economy.
- Musical identities are concentrated in **non-hostile** natives (Singer, Tuner, Echo Golem, Trill) and in the **Harmonizer**.
