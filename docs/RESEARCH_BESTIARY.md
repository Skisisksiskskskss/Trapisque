# RESEARCH — Bestiary of the Sift (Minecraft Dungeons II)

Split out of RESEARCH.md (WP-004). Facts only; design verdicts belong to Phase 2.

**Source key.**
- `[W:Page]` = minecraft.wiki page `Dungeons II:Page`, fetched 2026-09-30 (revisions in `research/wiki_pages_2026-09-30.tsv`).
- `[OBS]` = our own description of an official image that the wiki hosts (not wiki text).
- Press IDs (`[R-…]`, `[P-…]`, `[D-…]`) resolve in `research/press_sources_2026-09-30.md`.
- Each quote is followed by its source.

**Confidence**, the same scale as RESEARCH.md:
- **H**: official or in-game text we read, or an official image we looked at.
- **M**: unflagged wiki text, or first-hand (tier-B) press.
- **L**: flagged wiki text (conjecture, uncertain or delete), novel-only lore, fan tweets, a single tier-C/D source, or a name only.

A row's label covers its *existence and main facts*. Parts with lower confidence are marked inline.

**Naming.** Many species were known by other names before launch: wiki placeholders or developer codenames, not renames by Mojang (RESEARCH §7 #8). The current page title comes first; earlier names follow "aka".

## Portal guardian and invaders (non-native)
- **Twisted Warden** [W:Twisted Warden] **M**:
  - "a colossal warden boss found guarding the Deep Dark portal", a warden that was "affected by souls" and became "much bigger and much worse" [W:Twisted Warden].
  - Attacks: a beam, soul projectiles, and "erupting patches of soul fire all over the arena" [W:Twisted Warden].
  - It is on the Overworld side.
- **Illagers in the Sift** **M**:
  - The Enchanter, Pillager, Royal Guard and Vindicator are hostiles in Singer's Meadow, and the Ravager spawns in the Sift.
  - Camps: "Drive off the illagers camped near the Echo Den" [W:Achievement].
  - Leaders: the **High Council**. The Grand Illusioner opens rifts with its staff (M: [W:Grand Illusioner's Staff], [W:Rift]), and the Supreme Evoker soul-corrupts mobs. That they are empowered by **soul blocks** taken from echo golems is **L** (novel). [W:Singer's Meadow], [W:Royal Guard], [W:Illager], [W:High Council]
- **Soul corrupted** is a status, not a species [W:Soul Corrupted] **M**:
  - Corrupted mobs "glow, emit soul particles, and are far more resilient" [W:Soul Corrupted].
  - Corruption is a rare replacement with random properties (Bubble Shield, Burning, Freezing, Swiftness, Toxic and others) plus a soul-wave attack.
  - Affected Sift mobs include the Harmonizer, Hunter, Nester, Sentinel and Stalker.

## Cross-species patterns (our observations, not canon)
- **Two hostile ecologies:**
  - **Sifters**: plant-and-animal surface fauna, colourful, filling support, ranged and melee roles.
  - **Sculkers**: sculk hunters of the Carapace, using soul fire, afterimages and dashes.
- **A recurring support mechanic:** beams that **buff allies** (the Sprout, the Harmonizer), countered by killing the linked ally.
- **Souls appear as projectiles, fire and pools** (the Scavenger, the Sculk Monstrosity, the Twisted Warden), the hostile side of the soul economy.
- **Musical identities** are concentrated in **non-hostile** natives (the Singer, Tuner, Echo Golem and Trill) and in the **Harmonizer**.
- **Novel-only lore:** much of the character detail (the Singer, the echo golems' purpose, the visitors) comes only from the novel *The Rift*, so it is **L** for design purposes.
