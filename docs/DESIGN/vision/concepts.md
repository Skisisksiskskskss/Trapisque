# Vision concepts (WP-010) — research → diverge → converge

## 1. Research: what makes a dimension work
**Mojang's stated bar (canon quotes, RESEARCH.md S-W1):** jeb_ (2026-08-19): each dimension needs a functional **"reason to exist"**. Brandon Pearce (2022-11-22): a new dimension must **functionally distinguish itself** from existing ones.

**Vanilla precedents** (general Minecraft design knowledge, not Sift canon):
| Dimension | What works | What doesn't |
|---|---|---|
| Nether | One-line identity (fire and heat); a *reason to go* (blaze rods, quartz, glowstone, netherite, 8:1 travel); the player **builds** the entrance; every biome readable in seconds; rules visibly differ (water boils, beds explode, compasses spin) | Pre-1.16 it was barren; the reason to stay was thin until biomes and bastions |
| End | A **quest** to find it (eyes of ender teach themselves); a finale with ceremony (dragon, boss bar, credits); lasting rewards (elytra, shulker boxes) | After the dragon the main island is empty; the outer End repeats one biome |
| Deep Dark | **Rules through sound** (sensors, shriekers, warden) readable by watching; tension without constant combat | Pure avoidance loop — once looted, little reason to return |

**Lessons:** (1) the reason to exist must be a *verb the player can only do there*; (2) entry should be a self-teaching quest with a visible landmark; (3) rules should differ in ways the player can see and exploit; (4) rewards must be sidegrades or new capabilities, never skipping a vanilla tier; (5) plan the return visit (resources that renew, states that change).

**Prior art (RESEARCH.md §8):** three fan mods use the Ancient City for their portal. Only one runs on 26.3: Mielon's The Sift opens the frame with a note-block melody ("awaken it with sound"). Distinctness has to come from *what the Sift is for*, not only from how it is entered.

**What canon hands us (RESEARCH.md §1–5):** a dimension that is beautiful and teeming with souls; healthy sculk as its living ground; song as its language (Singer, note block machine, musical gate, Harmonizer); a three-Tide **time cycle** that changes soul flow and danger; natives who are companions and threats; Illagers stealing its souls; and in Dungeons II, a story portal at the Ancient City's centre. That last one is canon for Dungeons II only: Mojang hasn't said how Java will reach the Sift (RESEARCH.md §6).

## 2. Diverge — 14 vision concepts (written before scoring)
Each: a one-line fantasy · the verb only the Sift offers · what you bring home.

1. **Song Dimension** — "A world that listens and answers when you play." · Making sound shapes the world (sculk grows, gates open, creatures respond). · A way to use music as a tool.
2. **Soul Ecology** — "A living ecosystem that runs on souls." · Gathering, carrying and investing souls; everything alive feeds the flow. · Soul-powered utilities.
3. **Tide World** — "A dimension whose rules change on a clock you learn to read." · Planning around three Tides (bounty, neutral, danger). · Tide-bound resources.
4. **Healing the Sculk** — "Cure the disease that birthed the Deep Dark's corruption." · Restoring corrupted land; unlocking areas by healing. · A remedy that tames sculk at home.
5. **The Resistance** — "Drive the Illager occupation out of a world that isn't theirs." · Raiding soul-harvesting camps, freeing golems, retaking soul blocks. · Stolen Sift artifacts.
6. **Menagerie** — "A land of strange creatures that choose to follow you." · Befriending natives whose abilities open traversal. · Companions and pets.
7. **Frontier Expedition** — "A beautiful wilderness that is hardest to survive at night-Tide." · Long expeditions, landmarks, shelter-building for Endure. · Maps, charts, rare materials.
8. **Mirror of the Deep Dark** — "Where the Deep Dark's dread is turned inside out." · Every Deep Dark threat has a Sift counterpart that helps if you understand it (sound heals instead of summons). · Knowledge that makes the Deep Dark safer — at a cost.
9. **The Great Instrument** *(unusual)* — "The whole dimension is an instrument and you learn to play it." · Composing melodies on resonant terrain that reshape it (bridges bloom, walls part). · Musical building blocks.
10. **Inside a Living Giant** *(unusual)* — "You are walking inside a vast sleeping creature." · Carapace = shell, husks = bones, ichor = blood, sculk = nerves, Tides = its breathing; you learn its anatomy. · Organs as materials.
11. **Echo Navigation** *(unusual)* — "A land you cross by listening, not looking." · Sonar-like pings reveal paths in Endure darkness. · Sound-based navigation tools.
12. **Soul Storm Runs** — "Short, risky expeditions for soul treasure." · Timed runs between Tides with escalating danger. · Loot that only storms yield.
13. **Pastoral Haven** — "A calm, lovely place to build and tend strange gardens." (the novel's "peace and calm") · Cultivating Sift flora and pets. · A huge new building palette.
14. **The Singer's Choir** — "Earn a place in the choir that keeps the Sift alive." · Learning songs from natives step by step; each song is a new ability. · Songs that work in the Overworld.

Scoring notes (the calls that decided it): **Tide World** and **Mirror of the Deep Dark** are the only concepts with a *verb nobody else offers* that is also cheap to read (sky, music and spawns change together; sound means help here, danger there). **Song** and **Soul Ecology** are essential flavour but, alone, risk invisible numbers (readability 3). **Resistance/Menagerie/Frontier** are good *content* but not a reason to exist (distinct ≤3). The unusual concepts (9–11) are exciting but fight vanilla's grammar or can't be read without UI.

## 4. Pick — a merge
**Core = 8 (Mirror of the Deep Dark) + 3 (Tide World)**, carried by **1 (song) and 2 (souls)** as the mechanics that make them tangible; **5 (Illagers)** and **6 (companions)** supply conflict and friends as content. Borrow from 14 only the idea that songs are *learned from natives*; from 9 only the idea that some Sift blocks respond to notes. Cut 10–13 as directions (single ideas may survive in IDEAS.md).

Why this merge: it is the only combination where the Sift's reason to exist is a *pair of verbs* — **listen to the Tide, answer with sound** — that (a) no vanilla dimension offers, (b) comes straight from canon (time cycle; song opens gates; healthy sculk), (c) reuses vanilla grammar (sculk vibrations, note blocks, allays' love of music, day/night-like timelines), and (d) gives the Overworld something back (the Deep Dark read in a new way). Recorded as D-005.

**Changed after critique round 1 (WP-014).** Concept 8's reward, "knowledge that makes the Deep Dark safer", was cut. Sift tools and souls affect only Sift life, so the Ancient City stays exactly as dangerous (00_VISION §6, MF3). What the Overworld gets back is a new way to *read* the Deep Dark, not a way to disarm it. In Endure, the Sift's own hunters listen as wardens do.
