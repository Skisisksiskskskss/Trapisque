# The Sift — Vision

**Status:** FROZEN for Gate A (2026-09-30), after three critique rounds (WP-014). The §5.5 critique cap was applied after round 3; see §10. The owner can still change anything at Gate A.

## 1. Fantasy
**The Sift is the living heart the Deep Dark lost: a luminous world of healthy sculk that answers sound instead of punishing it, where light, creatures, souls and the ichor tide rise and fall with the Tides.**

## 2. Tone
- **Wonder first.** A teal sky over coral-orange grass and pale trees (the vanilla first look, D-002); flora that seems to glow; creatures that squeak, bounce, sing and stack into wobbling towers. The first minutes are beautiful and safe enough to look around.
- **The Deep Dark, inverted.** Down there, silence keeps you alive. Here, in Thrive, sound is how you are heard, welcomed and helped. The same sculk grammar the player already fears means something kinder. When Endure falls, the old rule returns: the hunters listen.
- **A song with a shadow.**
  - Each cycle the ichor tide rises and Endure falls: the light fails, the stars come out, and hunters walk.
  - The Illagers are here, draining souls from the ground. Around their rigs, healthy sculk sickens into pulsing blight, the colour of the Deep Dark. They feed an old disease; they didn't start it.
- **Vanilla restraint.** There are no dialogue windows and no quest log. Places tell the story: a caged echo golem, blight creeping from a harvester's rig, souls drifting into an Ancient City frame. Few new things, each doing a lot.

## 3. Pillars (every later decision is checked against these)
| # | Pillar | A feature passes if… |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Sound brings life**: the Sift listens and answers | the player can change it, or read it, through sound; that sound has a price or a risk somewhere (in Endure, the hunters hear it); and every audio cue has a visual and a subtitle |
| P2 | **The Tide sets the rules**: the world breathes on a readable cycle | it behaves differently in at least one Tide, and the difference can be **seen** without UI (sound may add to it, never replace it) |
| P3 | **Souls are the lifeblood**: life feeds the Sift, and you decide where your own souls go | souls visibly move because of it (drunk, offered, spent, freed or drained), and it never turns souls back into experience |
| P4 | **Wonder first, danger earned**: beauty up front, threat that telegraphs | a first-time player can survive their first Thrive by watching, and every lethal threat has a wind-up and a counter |

Tie-breakers: canon over invention; vanilla grammar over new UI; fewer, deeper features over more.

## 4. Why the Sift exists (the verbs no other dimension offers)
1. **Read the tide.** The Tides are literal: ichor rises and falls in the Sift's tide basins.
   - **Thrive** is low tide: the flats lie bare, with reagents you can gather only then, and life grows.
   - **Flow** is the tide moving. It has no effect on you (canon); you watch the waterline.
   - **Endure** is high tide: the flats flood, the hunters listen, and rare blooms open at the waterline.
2. **Answer with sound.** Note blocks, jukeboxes, goat horns and the Singer's gift of song spark growth, light and gates. Blubs gather and stack into teetering towers; echo golems dance. In Endure the same music is a lure for hunters.
3. **Tend the flow of souls.**
   - Deaths near a bloom heart feed healthy sculk, as a vanilla catalyst would.
   - Players *offer* their own experience, deliberately, to wake frames and make soul blocks. Soul blocks are fuel for golems, gates and gear traits.
   - Illagers drain souls, and blight creeps from their rigs.
   - Nothing turns souls back into experience.

**What comes home:**
- the gift of song (it acts on Sift life in the Sift; at home, Sift blocks and companions *react* to any music, as decoration);
- a large new building palette;
- companions (blubs, echo golems) running on soul blocks;
- soul-powered utilities;
- gear traits that work in the Sift;
- new music.

Nothing from the Sift grows, spreads or takes XP in the Overworld.

## 6. Progression placement
- **Requires:**
  - reaching an Ancient City, which is deep warden country, so in practice iron or diamond gear;
  - paying the frame's price: the experience of reaching level 30 from zero, offered deliberately, once per frame, which can be pooled.

  There is no Nether or End prerequisite. The Sift is a **mid-to-late side branch beside the Nether**, not a step before or after the End.
- **Gives:**
  - new *capabilities and sidegrades*: sound tools, soul utilities, companions, blocks and music;
  - **gear traits** added to the gear you already have, as a smithing template does.

  Traits add **Sift-only utility**, such as wading in ichor, reading the tide or muffling your steps from hunters. They work **only in the Sift**, and never add damage, protection, mining speed or durability. There is no new tier.
- **Never gives:**
  - flight (the elytra stays the End's);
  - bulk portable storage (shulkers);
  - any power above netherite: traits are Sift-only utility;
  - faster travel than the Nether (coordinate scale 1:1);
  - renewable diamonds or netherite;
  - **any way to create experience or turn souls back into it;**
  - **any power over wardens or the Deep Dark's sculk.**
- **Why vanilla stays intact:**
  - The Sift's rewards change *how* you play (sound, souls, Tides, friends) without skipping a vanilla milestone.
  - Every soul effect, the gift of song and every gear trait work only in the Sift (a scope flag, true in every Tide there). So the Ancient City stays exactly as dangerous, and no warden warning level ever follows a player home. Wardens can't cross the membrane.
  - Vanilla sculk placed anywhere behaves as vanilla.
  - The Overworld's weather is untouched (§8).
  - Resting in the Sift resets the phantom timer only during Endure, just as sleep does only at night.

## 7. Entry and return path (details and scoring: `vision/entry_path.md`, D-006/D-010)
**"Offer souls to wake it; music opens it."** 15 ideas were scored, and the revised design scores 29/35, ahead of rift-first (28). Rift-first also fails hard rule 9.4 and P4.
1. **Rumor.** A dormant frame breathes: souls drift from the city's sculk into its opening. It is cosmetic; nothing is taken.
2. **Offer.**
   - **Hold *use*** on the frame with an empty hand, and your XP streams in. Release and it stops. Nothing is taken any other way.
   - The price is 1 395 points (level 0 → 30), once per frame, and can be pooled. The charge shows in visible steps.
3. **Open.**
   - The awake frame drifts note particles. Any music before it fills the 20×6 opening with a cyan membrane: a note block, a goat horn, a jukebox (heard through its own listener), or later the gift of song.
   - The membrane shows the Tide on the far side.
   - The Tide clock waits at Thrive until the world's first crossing.
4. **Cross.**
   - Nether-style portal timing.
   - You arrive at an unbreakable **Sift-side gate** on a red-sculk hill, in the nearest Singer's Meadow within 256 blocks of the matching x/z, with a fallback. The link is stored explicitly.
   - The gate area is a sanctuary: no hostile spawns within ~16 blocks.
5. **Return.** Walk back through the gate to its linked frame. A removed membrane reopens with music. Rifts remain the Illagers' tool after 1.0, not the player's front door.

Why: it uses both canon ingredients (souls and music), each state teaches the next, it needs no new key item, and its cost is chosen, never taken. Canon places Dungeons II's story portal at the Ancient City's centre. Mojang hasn't said how Java will reach the Sift, so this is a design choice grounded in canon. Known interaction: if another mod's portal already fills the frame (Mielon's The Sift runs on 26.3), we stand aside.

## 8. The dimension's rules (full table and routes: `vision/rules.md`)
**Rule identity:** *the Tide sets the rules; everything else behaves like home.* The Sift is serene, not hostile, so the default precedent is the Overworld. Where vanilla treats every non-Overworld dimension alike, the Sift follows the Nether and End.
- **No day or night; the Tide instead.**
  - A 25-minute cycle keyframes sky light, sky and fog colour, stars (Endure only), motes and music on one timeline, and the tide basins follow it.
  - Surface monsters appear only when Endure darkens the land (vanilla light rules).
  - **No sun or moon** is drawn (both are parked at 180°). Clocks spin, as in the Nether and End. An inverted daylight detector senses Endure.
  - `/time set thesift:thrive` moves the Tide.
- **The hearing rule:** in Endure, each area's hunters hear the vibrations a sculk sensor hears, out to a warden's range of 16 blocks, plus jukeboxes within 10. A vibration particle shows each time they hear. At falling Flow they burrow away, so Thrive is safe.
- **Beds:**
  - Beds set your spawn, but nobody sleeps in beds or straw beds. Resting in a bed during Endure, with no monsters near, resets the phantom timer.
  - Nothing skips the Tide (the lever comes after 1.0, and moves only toward Thrive).
  - A charged respawn anchor explodes if used, as in the Overworld.
- **No weather, and the Overworld's weather is untouched.** Weather in 26.3 is server-global, so this takes one small mixin (D-008); the data-only alternative breaks maps. There are no raids, patrols or phantoms. Nether portals can't be lit. Coordinates are 1:1.
- Compasses spin; lodestone and recovery compasses work; maps work.
- **Ichor** (canon hazard): a thick liquid you wade through slowly (no swimming, no currents). It sets outsiders on fire, with soul flames, and drains their XP while they stand in it. Fire Resistance stops the burning but not the drain. Natives are immune, and some thrive in it (Mojang: "mobs that thrive through things that hurt the player"). It evaporates outside the Sift. In tide basins it rises and falls with the Tide.
- **Night-bound vanilla behaviour follows Endure:** bees stay home, eyeblossoms open, turtle eggs hatch faster, villagers rest.
- **Sculk:** blight is our own XP-free block family; vanilla sculk behaves as vanilla.
- **Code** is needed only for:
  - the weather mixin (the only mixin);
  - bed rest; ichor (its own block logic, no Entity mixin; D-013) and tide basins;
  - the hearing rule, the hunter retreat and enduring variants;
  - soul growth; the blight blocks;
  - the frame, gate and portal filter.

  Everything else is dimension-type, attribute and timeline data. No vanilla block or block entity is modified.

## 9. Core systems (concepts, scores and link map: `vision/systems.md`, D-007/D-011/D-012)
*Souls are the fuel, music is the spark, the Tide is the season, and ichor is the tide.*
- **The Tides.**
  - One global `thesift:tides` clock with a 30 000-tick (25-minute) cycle: **Thrive** (low tide) 10 min → **Flow** (rising) 2.5 → **Endure** (high tide) 10 → **Flow** (falling) 2.5.
  - Tide basins fill and drain with it, changing what is exposed, what grows and who hunts.
  - It is data-driven, with our own attributes: `thesift:gameplay/sift_life` (the scope gate: true in the Sift in every Tide, false elsewhere) and `thesift:gameplay/soul_flow` (a rate only: growth and song recharge). Sync, saving and `/time` come free.
  - The tide is a logical state per basin (each inside one chunk). Walling out the ichor doesn't keep the flats' reagents open.
  - At falling Flow, surface hunters burrow away (telegraphed), so Thrive is safe.
  - The clock waits at Thrive until the first crossing.
  - `advance_time false` freezes it like the sun. `random_tick_speed 0` freezes growth like crops.
- **Souls: a one-way ledger.**
  - Souls enter two ways: from non-player deaths near a **bloom heart**, which works like a vanilla catalyst but only in the Sift (in every Tide), and from deliberate **offerings** (hold *use*).
  - They leave as growth (blooms, light, reagents), as **soul blocks** (fuel, never a bank), or as a frame's price.
  - Thrive raises the growth *rate*, never the yield. Growth yields only common materials; tide-flat reagents and Endure blooms come only from the tide. Healthy sculk never drops XP, and no Sift item gives smelting XP.
  - Illager **soul tanks** hold what their rig drained; breaking one returns the souls to the land.
- **Healthy vs corrupted sculk.**
  - Healthy sculk is petalled, still and colourful, and it answers music.
  - **Blight** is its own block family with the Deep Dark's grammar: it pulses, its veins creep, its tendrils listen, it shrieks. It never drops XP and never touches the warden tracker.
  - It spreads only around active Illager rigs, capped, and stops when the rig falls.
  - Souls, song or freed golems cure it, but only in the Sift (the scope gate), never in the Overworld's Deep Dark.
- **Sound.**
  - Music sparks growth, opens gates, and makes blubs stack into towers (canon) while echo golems dance.
  - **The hearing rule:** Nesters in the Meadow hear, in Endure, the vibrations a sculk sensor hears, out to a warden's 16 blocks, plus jukeboxes within 10. A vibration particle is the tell. Sneaking and wool work as in vanilla.
  - The Singer's **gift of song** is earned by restoring the Meadow's stolen harmony. Its charges refill twice as fast in Thrive and not at all in Endure.
  - Every sound has a visual and a subtitle; musical gates show their key as a carved instrument glyph.
- **The Illager occupation.**
  - Finite camps. While a camp's rig stands, blight creeps and its tanks fill. Break the rig and the creep stops; break the tanks and the land heals. Their grounds keep spawning pillagers only, as outposts do (no evokers, no totems).
  - Freed echo golems carry soul blocks and **heal blight** around them.
  - Endure's hunters attack camps, a war the player can steer with sound.
  - The **Illager Keep** and **rifts** come after 1.0.
- **Scope for 1.0 (fewer, deeper): one region, Singer's Meadow**, with Lullaby Hills and the tide basins. Every item is tiered (systems.md §7):
  - **Must:** the Tides with basins and the hunter retreat; ichor; the entry, gate and return; healthy sculk, blight, bloom hearts and soul blocks; the gift of song and the **Singer**; **Blub**, **Echo Golem**, **Nester**, **Bloombud**; Illager camps; **one boss, the Sculk Monstrosity**.
  - **Should:** Pollinator and Sprout, the Slabber, the **Harmonizer** miniboss, musical gates, gear traits, Tide music.
  - **Could:** blub towers as platforms, home-decoration reactions, Trills, a larger palette.

  **1.1** brings the Carapace, its sculkers and the Monarch. The Keep, rifts, the Tide lever and an aurora sky come later still.
- **Everything connects:**
  - Tides → the waterline, light, growth rate, what sound does;
  - souls → growth, soul blocks → golems, gates, traits;
  - Illagers → blight → cured by souls, song or golems;
  - sound → growth, natives, gates, hunters.
