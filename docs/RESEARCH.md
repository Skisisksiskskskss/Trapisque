# RESEARCH — The Sift canon

Every claim cites a source. **Confidence:** H = official (Mojang/Xbox) text, in-game text, or an official image we looked at · M = minecraft.wiki summary without a conjecture flag · L = conjecture-flagged, novel-only, single secondary source, or name-only. "Conjecture" = wiki editors' naming, not Mojang's. Bestiary: `RESEARCH_BESTIARY.md`. Official/press quotes: section 9 (WP-003 part 2).

## 2. Areas (Dungeons II) — look and contents
- **Singer's Meadow** — "red sculk, teal grass, and blue trees"; "red sculk blocks beneath tall blue grass"; green flowers, yellow flower patches, pale-blue flowers, "towering tree-like growths covered in pale-blue vines"; "many pools of ichor, fracturing the terrain"; bounded by "walls of darker red sculk"; the portal sits on a small hill of red sculk. Home of the Singer and echo golems; passives Blub, Echo Golem, Slabber, Trill; hostiles Bloombud, Nester, Pollinator, Seedling + illagers. First visit unlocks the Tides tutorial. [W:Singer's Meadow] M. Our view of the portal image [S-I4]: mauve/rose canyon walls, glowing cyan grass, icy-cyan drooping trees, bright cyan portal, blocky teal/pink aurora sky. H
- **The Carapace** — "a flat, dry biome composed of dark blue stone and vast fields of sand and dust", chasms and ravines, occasional ichor pools, "red and yellow grass patches", "bouncy slimes used to access higher areas", "colossal fossils … the largest of these are the humbler husks, which can be entered". Home of sculkers (Stalker, Scavenger, Monarch). Sub-area **Echo Den** (echo golems, illager camp nearby). [W:The Carapace], [W:Echo Den] M. Image [S-I5]: blue-teal stone pillars, pale sand, magenta and yellow coral-like grass, same aurora sky. H
- **Lullaby Hills** — "contains echo golems"; quest to wake them ("Choir Conductor"). Nothing else known. [W:Lullaby Hills] M
- **Hinted but undocumented:** Humbler Huskland (listed location); soundtrack areas Wild Meadows, Ravaged Meadow, Radiant Ravines, Mites, Hollowed Island, Illager Stronghold, Illager Keep (Tide-suffixed tracks). [W:Location], [W:Original Game Soundtrack] L

## 3. Tides (what is known, what isn't)
- Known: three Tides in a **time cycle**; Flow = no effect; Thrive = faster soul regeneration + faster artifact cooldowns; Endure = more powerful mobs spawn + passive soul regeneration stops; they affect traversal; each has a distinct look (day-like / golden / night-like) and area music variants.
- Unknown: order, duration, triggers other than time, whether all areas shift together, what "traversal" changes mean mechanically.
- Implication (analysis, not canon): the Tides behave like the Sift's own day/night — a natural fit for 26.3 world clocks + timelines (VANILLA_ANALOGS W4).

## 4. Souls, sculk, sound
- **Souls** are the Sift's key resource; illagers came for them; the Singer gathers them by singing and condenses them into soul blocks; ichor and soul fire drain them; sculker attacks weaponise them (soul projectiles, soul-fire pools, soul-flame charges). [W:Soul], [W:Soul Block], [W:Ichor], [W:Scavenger], [W:Sculk Monstrosity] M
- **Vanilla hook:** sculk catalysts already turn a nearby death's XP into sculk charge and "bloom" `sculk_soul` particles (VANILLA_ANALOGS E4). [S-J1 sources] H
- **Healthy vs corrupted sculk:** corrupted sculk is a disease of the Sift's healthy sculk; healthy sculk thrives there. The vanilla first look shows healthy sculk as green/orange/pink ground and grass. No text describes gameplay differences. [W:Sculk], [V:The Sift] M
- **Sound/music** is structural: the portal is opened by a melody (note block machine) or a Singer's song; a "musical gate" blocks the sculker nest; the Harmonizer attacks with shockwave screeches; singer vocals are used as instruments in the soundtrack; names (Lullaby, Choir, Tuner, Harmonizer, Opus, Crescendo). [W:Deep Dark Portal], [W:Singer], [W:Wrath of the Sculkers], [W:Harmonizer], [W:Original Game Soundtrack] M → the owner's §3.2 observation 1 is **supported**.

## 5. Palette evidence and the colour contradiction
- "Red and orange stone, green vegetation" (wiki, dimension-wide) and "a vivid mix of neon blue and pink" (press, §3.1) are **both consistent with the images**: terrain mass is rose-red/orange stone (redder in Thrive, orange in Flow, near-black purple in Endure); foliage and glow accents are cyan/teal/blue; skies are teal with pink/green blocky auroras; the Carapace swaps in blue stone and pale sand with magenta/yellow grass. "Neon" on the wiki appears only for soul-corrupted Overworld mobs (reveal trailer), not for Sift terrain. [W:Reveal Trailer], [S-I3], [S-I4], [S-I5] H (images)
- **Varies by Tide and by area** — the owner's §3.2 observation 6 is **supported**.
- **Vanilla-specific:** the Java/Bedrock first look is pinker and flatter (turquoise sky, coral grass, pale trees) and is the highest authority for *our* art direction (D-002). Dungeons II images are mood reference only; nothing is traced or reused (§9 rule 1).

## 7. Contradictions
1. Colour wording (neon blue & pink vs red/orange stone) — resolved in §5.
2. Harmonizer's mission "Wading Rainy Plains": Sift (Harmonizer page) vs Overworld (Quest page). [W:Harmonizer], [W:Quest]
3. Boss list: the Sift page names Harmonizer a boss but files it under minibosses. [W:The Sift]
4. Illager origin: one echo golem killed [W:Soul Block] vs three [W:Illager].
5. Monarch location: sculker nest in the Carapace vs quest filed under Singer's Meadow. [W:Monarch], [W:Wrath of the Sculkers]
6. Novel tone ("a wondrous dimension of peace and calm") vs marketing ("threats, mysteries, and breathtaking beauty"). [W:The Rift], [W:The Sift]
7. Species names changed at launch (Licker→Slabber, Sculk Mage→Scavenger, Sculk Slasher→Stalker, Snout Sifter→Bloombud, Groobler Sentinel→Pollinator); the §3.1 seed list uses old names. [W:* redirects]
