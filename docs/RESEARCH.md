# RESEARCH — The Sift canon

Every claim cites a source. **Confidence:** H = official (Mojang/Xbox) text, in-game text, or an official image we looked at · M = minecraft.wiki summary without a conjecture flag · L = conjecture-flagged, novel-only, single secondary source, or name-only. "Conjecture" = wiki editors' naming, not Mojang's. Bestiary: `RESEARCH_BESTIARY.md`. Official/press quotes: section 9 (WP-003 part 2).

## 2. Areas (Dungeons II) — look and contents
- **Singer's Meadow** — "red sculk, teal grass, and blue trees"; "red sculk blocks beneath tall blue grass"; green flowers, yellow flower patches, pale-blue flowers, "towering tree-like growths covered in pale-blue vines"; "many pools of ichor, fracturing the terrain"; bounded by "walls of darker red sculk"; the portal sits on a small hill of red sculk. Home of the Singer and echo golems; passives Blub, Echo Golem, Slabber, Trill; hostiles Bloombud, Nester, Pollinator, Seedling + illagers. First visit unlocks the Tides tutorial. [W:Singer's Meadow] M. Our view of the portal image [S-I4]: mauve/rose canyon walls, glowing cyan grass, icy-cyan drooping trees, bright cyan portal, blocky teal/pink aurora sky. H
- **The Carapace** — "a flat, dry biome composed of dark blue stone and vast fields of sand and dust", chasms and ravines, occasional ichor pools, "red and yellow grass patches", "bouncy slimes used to access higher areas", "colossal fossils … the largest of these are the humbler husks, which can be entered". Home of sculkers (Stalker, Scavenger, Monarch). Sub-area **Echo Den** (echo golems, illager camp nearby). [W:The Carapace], [W:Echo Den] M. Image [S-I5]: blue-teal stone pillars, pale sand, magenta and yellow coral-like grass, same aurora sky. H
- **Lullaby Hills** — "contains echo golems"; quest to wake them ("Choir Conductor"). Nothing else known. [W:Lullaby Hills] M
- **Hinted but undocumented:** Humbler Huskland (listed location); soundtrack areas Wild Meadows, Ravaged Meadow, Radiant Ravines, Mites, Hollowed Island, Illager Stronghold, Illager Keep (Tide-suffixed tracks). [W:Location], [W:Original Game Soundtrack] L

## 3. Tides (what is known, what isn't)
- Known: three Tides in a **time cycle**; Flow = no effect; Thrive = faster soul regeneration + faster artifact cooldowns; Endure = more powerful mobs spawn + passive soul regeneration stops; they affect traversal; each has a distinct look (day-like / golden / night-like) and area music variants.
- Unknown: order, duration, triggers other than time, whether all areas shift together, what "traversal" changes mean mechanically.
- Implication (analysis, not canon): the Tides behave like the Sift's own day/night — a natural fit for 26.3 world clocks + timelines (VANILLA_ANALOGS W4).

## 4. Souls, sculk, sound
- **Souls** are the Sift's key resource; illagers came for them; the Singer gathers them by singing and condenses them into soul blocks; ichor and soul fire drain them; sculker attacks weaponise them (soul projectiles, soul-fire pools, soul-flame charges). [W:Soul], [W:Soul Block], [W:Ichor], [W:Scavenger], [W:Sculk Monstrosity] M
- **Vanilla hook:** sculk catalysts already turn a nearby death's XP into sculk charge and "bloom" `sculk_soul` particles (VANILLA_ANALOGS E4). [S-J1 sources] H
- **Healthy vs corrupted sculk:** corrupted sculk is a disease of the Sift's healthy sculk; healthy sculk thrives there. The vanilla first look shows healthy sculk as green/orange/pink ground and grass. No text describes gameplay differences. [W:Sculk], [V:The Sift] M
- **Sound/music** is structural: the portal is opened by a melody (note block machine) or a Singer's song; a "musical gate" blocks the sculker nest; the Harmonizer attacks with shockwave screeches; singer vocals are used as instruments in the soundtrack; names (Lullaby, Choir, Tuner, Harmonizer, Opus, Crescendo). [W:Deep Dark Portal], [W:Singer], [W:Wrath of the Sculkers], [W:Harmonizer], [W:Original Game Soundtrack] M → the owner's §3.2 observation 1 is **supported**.

## 5. Palette evidence and the colour contradiction — resolved
- **The two §3.1 descriptions describe different things.** "A vivid mix of neon blue and pink" is how press described the **vanilla Java/Bedrock teaser** shown at LIVE ("pink grass beneath a turquoise sky" [P-PCGAMER]; "candy-colored pink and turquoise" [P-80LV]); Game Rant states both versions side by side [P-GRANT]. "Red and orange stone, green vegetation, ichor, and sculk" is the wiki's line about the **Dungeons II** Sift, repeated by guides [P-GRANT, P-SK1, P-SK2]. **H**
- **Dungeons II shows at least two palettes** in official images: (1) rose/crimson stone + icy-turquoise foliage + aurora sky (official alt text: "Glowing blue portal surrounded by icy turquoise vegetation in a colorful canyon landscape." [MC1]); (2) orange/terracotta stone + green vegetation + purple and rainbow liquids (Xbox Wire screenshot `Wellspring_01` [XW3]). Our own look at the Tide stills adds that Thrive is pastel rose/cyan, Flow is orange, and Endure is dark purple with a blue glow [S-I3]. So the palette **varies by area and by Tide**. **H** (images) / M (area attribution)
- Official adjectives: "colorful new look" [XW1]; "beautiful, vibrant, and teeming with souls" [MC1]; "unique hues and pastel-infused design" [XW3]; reviewers: "blue-pink world" [R-KOT], "Nether in the negative" [R-GT], "coral-hued environments peppered with inviting yet corrosive pools" [R-GA].
- On the wiki, "neon" appears only for soul-corrupted Overworld mobs in the reveal trailer, never for Sift terrain [W:Reveal Trailer].
- **For our art direction:** the vanilla teaser (turquoise sky, coral/salmon grass, pale trees, pale-blue Blub) is the highest authority (D-002). Dungeons II images are mood reference only; nothing is traced or reused (§9 rule 1).

## 7. Contradictions
1. Colour wording (neon blue & pink vs red/orange stone) — resolved in §5.
2. Harmonizer's mission "Wading Rainy Plains": Sift (Harmonizer page) vs Overworld (Quest page). [W:Harmonizer], [W:Quest]
3. Boss list: the Sift page names Harmonizer a boss but files it under minibosses. [W:The Sift]
4. Illager origin: one echo golem killed [W:Soul Block] vs three [W:Illager].
5. Monarch location: sculker nest in the Carapace vs quest filed under Singer's Meadow. [W:Monarch], [W:Wrath of the Sculkers]
6. Novel tone ("a wondrous dimension of peace and calm") vs marketing ("threats, mysteries, and breathtaking beauty"). [W:The Rift], [W:The Sift]
7. Species names changed at launch (Licker→Slabber, Sculk Mage→Scavenger, Sculk Slasher→Stalker, Snout Sifter→Bloombud, Groobler Sentinel→Pollinator); the §3.1 seed list uses old names. [W:* redirects]

## 9. Official and press sources (WP-003 part 2)
90 sources read (22 official, 20 reviews/interviews, 36 news/guides, 12 low-reliability, which we cite only as leads); 558 quoted passages were machine-checked against the saved page text, with 0 mismatches. Key additions:
- **Design intent (the strongest guidance we have):** Måns Olson: the Nether and End "are very hostile and imposing… For the Sift… [Mojang] 'really wanted to make something that felt a bit peaceful - serene, almost - and that gave a very different color palette'… 'it's still a dangerous place for the player to be… but it's not evil.'" [I-PCG1] **B**. Laura De Llorens (Design Director): "The world has its own independent ecosystem. There's mobs that thrive through things that hurt the player, for instance, but the mobs live there. They're adapted to that." / "…didn't feel necessarily threatening or necessarily very benevolent. It's just its own independent thing" [XW2] **H**.
- **Rules:** "once you enter, new rules to learn. Shifting tides within the Sift can affect your character and those around you; hazardous new blocks will alter how you approach exploration" [XW1] **H**. Reviewers: the Sift "flips, visually and mechanically, between different states… easier in the Thrive… more punishing mobs… when the Endure tide hits" [R-PCG]; "time-limited, global modifiers" [R-GRP]; "the water in the Sift will set you on fire" [R-DS] **B**.
- **Tides replace day/night:** the official soundtrack gives Sift areas (Thrive)/(Flow)/(Endure) versions of each track, where Overworld areas get (Sunrise)/(Day)/(Sunset)/(Night) [OST] **H** (fact) / M (reading).
- **Access in Dungeons II:** rifts are the everyday way in ("lots of rifts that appear throughout the world. If you do stumble across and then into one, it will transport you to the Sift." [MC1]; rift runs are short and close behind you [R-KOT]). The Deep Dark portal is the story portal, opened with the Note Block Machine ("Note Block Virtuoso", held by 2.9% of Steam players versus 77.7% for "Brave the Unknown" [ST3]). **H**
- **Java/Bedrock:** "coming to Minecraft in 2027… Mojang isn't talking too much about how to access the Sift, or how it will stand out from Dungeons II's expression of it" [XW1] **H**.
- **Creatures:** the Blub is the mascot, "like the Ghast in the Nether or the Endermen of the End" [P-GRANT]; Blubs "jump on top of one another to form teetering towers that occasionally topple over" [R-KOT] **B**; in the vanilla clip it walks rather than hops [P-PCGAMER]. Echo golems read as "dancing automatons roaming about" [R-IGN]. The Singer is described (novel previews) with "pale green bodies, shaggy fur, tiny faces on their elongated necks, pale antlers, short legs, and long arms" [P-BB1] **L**. Creatures "seemingly conversing through melodies" [R-GA].
- **Lore:** an "environmental crisis" plus a clear enemy, the Illager High Council (Prime Enchanter, Supreme Evoker, Grand Illusioner), who "take control of Souls" [XW2, MC7, R-GT] **H/B**. Side quest "Breaking Camps" (illager camps) [P-GSC2]; track titles "Caged", "Soul Crisis", "Song of Healing", "Illager Stronghold/Keep" [OST].
- **Endgame in Dungeons II:** Soul Storms (timed zones with stronger mobs and generators to destroy) [I-PCG2, R-PCG]. This is Dungeons-specific; parked as an idea.
- **Contradictions found here:** one guide says difficulty rises through Flow→Thrive→Endure [P-BB4], against four sources saying Thrive is the easy Tide [R-PU, P-BB3, R-PCG, R-GRP]; Singer colour green vs gray (low-reliability source); "Sniffer in the Sift" [P-AG] is probably a misidentified Licker/Slabber.
- **Not reachable:** GameSpot review and interviews, TheSixthAxis, Neowin, Mobalytics, YouTube descriptions/transcripts (CAPTCHA). No official patch notes exist.
